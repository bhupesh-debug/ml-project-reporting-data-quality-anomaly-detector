# Corporate Finance Business Case: Reporting Data-Quality Anomaly Detector

## 1. Investment Thesis & Financial Impact
Inaccurate journal transactions entering external financial or regulatory reports trigger audit restatements, legal discovery expenses, regulatory enforcement fines, and expensive month-end close firefighting.

## 3. Benefit Methodology & Assumptions
1. **Avoided Regulatory Penalties ($1.85M in Base Case)**: Historical audit fees for material weakness remediation and external auditor forensic procedures average $1.2M - $2.5M. Eliminating material feed corruptions avoids these non-routine professional services fees.
2. **Avoided Close Firefighting ($520k in Base Case)**: Prevents emergency contractor hiring and late nights during the 5-day close window across 12 legal entities.
3. **Analyst Productivity ($140k in Base Case)**: Automates manual spreadsheet scrolling across 24,000 rows, saving 1,400 hours of accounting operations time ($100/hr loaded rate).
